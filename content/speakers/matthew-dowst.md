Matthew Dowst is a seasoned automation consultant and Microsoft MVP in both PowerShell and Azure Hybrid & Migration, bringing over 20 years of experience in IT management and consulting. Specializing in PowerShell for the last 15 years, he has helped clients of all sizes streamline and automate their production workloads.

Matthew is highly active in the PowerShell community, contributing through his blog, authoring the PowerShell Weekly newsletter, and publishing several widely-used modules on the PowerShell Gallery. He is also the author of Practical Automation with PowerShell, a comprehensive guide on enterprise-level automation, and serves as a board member of the Dallas Fort Worth Systems Management User Group (DFWSMUG). Matthew has presented at various conferences, including PowerShell and DevOps Global Summit, System Center Universe, and Innovate, sharing his insights and methodologies in automation and cloud technologies.
